# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

## Overview

This privacy policy describes how Ronnie Eichler ("I", "me") handles information on this website and through apps that I create.

## Website

This is a personal website. I do not knowingly collect personal information from visitors. The site may use basic server logs that record IP addresses and page requests, which is standard for web hosting. I do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

## App — Bike Ride Notifications

### Information Collected

- **Phone numbers**: Provided by me to send ride notifications to friends and family.

### How Information Is Used

Phone numbers are used solely to send SMS notifications when a bike ride starts or ends. These notifications include a link to a third-party tracking service where recipients can view the rider's location.

### Third-Party Services

- SMS messages are delivered through [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy), which processes phone numbers as necessary to deliver messages.
- Ride tracking links point to [Hammerhead](https://www.hammerhead.io), which has its own privacy policy governing location data and map usage.

### SMS Messaging

- Recipients receive 2-4 SMS messages per bike ride (start and stop notifications).
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time.
- Reply HELP for support information.

### Data Retention

Phone numbers are retained as long as a recipient remains an active contact in the App. Recipients who opt out by replying STOP have their phone number removed.

## Data Sharing

I do not sell, trade, or share personal information with third parties beyond what is described above.

## Security

I take reasonable measures to protect any stored information from unauthorized access.

## Children's Privacy

This website and App are not intended for use by children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
